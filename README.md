# Speech Pace

A live words-per-minute gauge and a timed ramp drill for speech practice.

**Use it here: https://joseangelo510.github.io/speech-pace/**

Open it in Chrome (Edge and Safari work too), allow the microphone, press **Start** and talk.

- **Free talk** shows your live pace, your average and peak, and a chart of the session.
- **Ramp up** raises the target pace by a chosen percentage every 20, 30, 45 or 60 seconds (or any interval you set), and scores each level against its target.

Words are counted by the browser's built-in speech recognition. In Chrome that means your audio is sent to Google while a session is running. The app itself records nothing; your session results are saved only in your own browser.

To run it on your Mac without the internet address, double-click `Speech Pace.command`.
